Where the work happened

CPSec Lab, Georgia Tech

Graduate researcher · August 2026–present

Cyber-physical security of marine and ocean-sensing infrastructure, advised by Prof. Saman Zonouz, with day-to-day supervision from Anna Raymaker (PhD student).

  • Built five zgrab2 scan modules — NMEA 0183, NMEA 2000 across five wire encodings, Signal K, SunSpec over Modbus, and vendor web interfaces — for a census of internet-exposed marine and operational-technology systems across some 6,000 hosts. They were developed and scored against a simulated SunSpec device network with manifest ground truth before any real host was contacted.
  • Found a 21.5% classification gap in a commercial device-search index: 760 hosts carry NMEA sentences in banners the index never labelled as NMEA. A receive-only decode of 283 live feeds recovered 11,584 unique vessel identities across 138 flag states, transmitting nothing.
  • Closed the question of whether NMEA 2000 reaches the internet directly, as a bounded negative: passive banner analysis, down-conversion cadence and diversity tests, and an authorised, allowlist-gated active probe over 200 feeds added no new confirmations.
  • Built an inversion that recovers coastal surface current from the kinematics of passing vessels, validated against HF-radar fields over seven region-windows in two seasons. The pre-registered replication gate failed, and the result is the better one: a characterised error floor near 0.2 m/s, with correlation monotone in signal-to-noise — the threshold below which a corrupted field cannot be caught by physics.

Topics: cyber-physical systems security, data assimilation and inverse problems, signal processing and SDR, fluid dynamics, systems and cloud.

MPCP

Lead founding engineer · 2025–2026

  • Built a six-stage perception-to-reasoning pipeline for real-time sports coaching — YOLOv8 detection, RTMPose keypoints, multi-person tracking, ST-GCN action embeddings, vLLM reasoning — running at twice real time on CPU.
  • Replaced the hand-written sport-specific heuristics with an evidence-first architecture: ST-GCN embeddings pretrained on NTU RGB+D (60 action classes, 56k sequences) are passed to the reasoning stage as structured evidence packets, with persistent temporal state and confidence decay, so each decision can be traced back to observable body mechanics.
  • Validated across two ACC competition days with five simultaneous athletes, holding swimmer identity through occlusion and lane changes via FastReID over six-hour sessions without human intervention.
  • Provisioned the AWS infrastructure from zero (VPC, IAM Identity Center, EC2, RDS, ALB/ACM, Docker Compose) across dev, prod and field, and led a team of five.

Topics: pose estimation and tracking, computer vision, graph neural networks, LLMs and multi-agent systems, systems and cloud.

Topics: generative models and diffusion, deep learning, probability.

ToSSim — emergent misalignment in multi-agent social deduction
